# Epic: Ragnarok Brawler (MVP)

> **Source**: `design/game-brief.md` (implicit epic — minimal tier, no `/create-epics`)
> **Engine**: Godot 4.7.2 (GDScript)

**Goal**: A pixel-art beat 'em up inspired by Ragnarok — fast combo-driven arcade combat against hordes, a mini boss and a boss.

**Scope**: the brief's MVP list — 1 Swordsman-style class, basic/heavy/dodge/skill, combo cancelling, 3–5 common enemies, 1 mini boss, 1 boss (2+ phases), 1 linear stage.

**Ordering**: the brief's Build order — movement/attack feel first, then combos, horde AI, mini boss, boss, stage.

**Out of scope**: multiplayer, extra classes, equipment/loot, exploration, persistent progression, story beyond framing, any official Ragnarok names/assets.
